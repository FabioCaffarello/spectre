# Code of Conduct

Spectre adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][cc] as its code
of conduct. By participating in this project, you agree to abide by its
terms.

The full text is available at:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Reporting

To report a concern, contact the project maintainer:

- **Email:** fabio.caffarello@gmail.com

Reports are reviewed by the maintainer and handled with discretion.
Reporters' identities are kept confidential to the extent possible
under applicable law.
